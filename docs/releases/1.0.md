# Panelgrove 1.0

Version code **9**; Android package `dev.soto.panelgrove`; Android **8.0+**.

Panelgrove 1.0 adds private, local reading statistics to the offline manga reader. No account, internet access, ads or analytics are required.

## New in 1.0

- **Reading time:** Weekly and monthly graphs, earlier-period comparisons and daily details. Open a series, volume or chapter to explore its sessions.
- **Reading activity:** Pages viewed, volumes and chapters finished, rereads, reading days, current and longest streaks, and a yearly activity calendar.
- **Your reading habits:** Most-read series and books, session counts and durations, language filters and language breakdowns.
- **Timer controls:** Pause or resume from the reader and choose an inactivity limit in Settings. Loading, covering menus, background use and screen locking do not accrue reading time.
- **Portable statistics:** Reading records travel with JSON backups. Older backups remain importable; repeated restores merge records without counting them twice.
- **Reader reliability:** Corrected vertical-scroll restoration during layout and strengthened checks for the visible reading position after recreation.

Language-edition stacks, independent progress per edition, page export, custom covers, contrast/gamma controls and CB7/7Z support from 0.9 remain included.

## Updating from 0.9 or 0.1.6

Export a JSON backup first and keep your manga files separately. Install the **publicly signed** 1.0 APK over the existing public app to retain its library, reading positions, bookmarks, history and settings. Do not uninstall as part of a normal update.

Reading statistics start with this update; earlier opening history cannot reconstruct past reading time or pages. A page counts after two seconds of visible reading. Manually marking a book Read does not create a dated finish. Checkpoints are saved every 15 seconds and on pause/exit; abrupt termination can lose the latest unsaved interval.

New backups include statistics and require Panelgrove 1.0 or newer to restore. Version 1 backups from older releases remain supported. Custom cover images, manga files and Android file-access grants are excluded from JSON backups; choose covers and grant folder access again when moving to a fresh installation.

Local preview/debug APKs use different signing keys and cannot update a public installation. Follow the [signing-transition guide](https://github.com/SotoOnGit/PanelGrove/blob/v1.0/docs/USAGE.md#moving-from-a-local-preview-or-debug-build) when switching from those builds.

## Known limits

- Custom cover images stay on the current installation and are not backed up.
- Encrypted/multipart archives, EPUB and unlisted 7Z codecs such as BZip2 remain unsupported.
- PDF page exports preserve the full page within a 2048 × 3072 pixel limit.
- Statistics are estimates of visible foreground reading, not proof of attention. Chapter breakdowns depend on document metadata. Goals and time-remaining estimates are outside 1.0.
